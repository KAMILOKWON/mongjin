Mongjin (몽진, 蒙塵) is an original two-player strategy board game from Korea. Escort your King to the far side of the board while your Guards open routes, block the opponent, and protect every step forward.

Simple rules lead to deep decisions. A Guard that protects your King can become your sharpest attacker, and one well-timed block can decide the match.

Features

• Quick Match with real-time online opponents
• Friend matches using a six-character room code
• Computer opponents with Easy, Normal, and Hard difficulty
• Local two-player matches on one device
• A step-by-step tutorial for new players
• Anonymous profiles with wins, losses, win rate, Elo rating, and ranking

Win by reaching the goal row with your King, capturing the opposing King, or surrounding it so it cannot move.

Find the balance between safety and speed, attack and defense, then lead your King home.
